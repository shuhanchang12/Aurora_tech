# Aurora Tech Chromebook & GPU AI Supply Chain Platform (Project: Atomic-Link)

🎓 RNCP 38777 AI Architect Master Defense Portfolio & Code Repository

This portfolio represents the complete, production-grade technical implementation and defense materials for Project: Atomic-Link, deployed at the fictional multinational hardware OEM, Aurora Tech Computing Group.

The system unifies macroeconomic currency exposure (EUR/USD/TWD) with real-time physical logistics data to predict product gross margin compression. It introduces a cutting-edge Ocean-to-Air conversion strategy: when critical NVIDIA GPUs are delayed at sea beyond a 10-day threshold, the system simulates expediting cargo via Air France Cargo to Paris CDG, cutting transit delay from 12 days to 2 days but driving freight costs from a standard €5 to €45 per unit, which triggers an automated financial margin warning.

Additionally, as a highly forward-looking strategic scaling path, the platform introduces a Web3 Smart Contract Hedging & USDC Settlement framework. When the AI engine predicts an imminent margin degradation of over 80%, it programmatically triggers an on-chain escrow contract. This contract converts Euro treasury assets to USD Coin (USDC) and locks the settlement rate with silicon and GPU suppliers instantly, bypassing traditional SWIFT payment latencies (3-5 business days) and mitigating severe intra-week foreign exchange fluctuations.
